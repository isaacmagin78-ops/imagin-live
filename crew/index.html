Straight answers

Questions, answered honestly.

Is it paid?

It depends on your path, and every deal is custom. Clients get a quote for exactly what they need and see the full price before any work starts. Crew is an internship or a barter, and we'll be clear up front about what is and isn't paid. Co-creator deals are worked out one-on-one.

Nothing is promised: not money, not followers, not results. You'll know what's on the table before you say yes.

What's barter?

A skills swap instead of cash. You help with IMagin's and Tyson's Time social media (filming, editing, posting, replying, ideas), and IMagin builds your side business in return, like your landing page, QR code and Shorts. We agree on who does what before anyone starts.

Do I need followers?

No. Zero followers is fine. We care about your side thing, your energy, and whether you show up. Already know social media? Great. Want to learn? Also great. Just tell us which.

Is AI used?

Yes, and we say so. IMagin uses AI tools to make things like 11-second AI spokesperson Shorts and AI front desks, and to help with editing and writing. AI-made content is disclosed, and we never pass off an AI voice or face as a real person.

Tyson and Miss T are 100% real. No fake reviews, no fake testimonials, no made-up numbers.

Where does this happen? Is it safe?

Pompano Beach and around South Florida. Walks with Tyson happen in public places, and you can bring a friend. Client work and some crew work can be done remotely.
