Before you start: turn on Siri AI (Beta)
  1. Update to iOS 27: Settings > General > Software Update.
  2. Open Settings > Siri and tap Try Siri AI (Beta). If Siri is off, tap Turn on Siri first.
  3. Pick a voice and finish voice setup. Apple puts you on a waitlist, and Siri AI downloads by itself when it's your turn. Wait times vary.
  • Needs iPhone 15 Pro, iPhone 15 Pro Max, any iPhone 16 or later, or iPhone Air.
  • Siri and your iPhone language must both be set to English for now. French, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese and Spanish are in the iOS 27.2 beta.
  • Not available on iPhone in the EU or mainland China yet. Not for users under 13.
  • Apple sets daily usage limits and says expanded access will cost a fee in the future.
  • Prefer the old Siri? You can switch back to Siri Classic in Siri settings.

Good to know

What's beta, what's coming

  • Apple labels all of Siri AI “Beta” in iOS 27, which shipped Sep 14, 2026.
  • iOS 27.2, in beta now, adds French, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese and Spanish.
  • Apple says Siri will soon work with Microsoft Outlook, Notability and Tripsy.
  • Under the hood, Apple says its new models were “custom-built in collaboration with Google and its Gemini models,” running on device and on Private Cloud Compute.

Reported, not live

  • Code found in iOS 27 suggests Claude could plug into Siri as a built-in extension, like ChatGPT. As of Oct 17, 2026, only the ChatGPT extension is available. Today, Claude works through the Claude app's “Ask Claude” shortcut.
