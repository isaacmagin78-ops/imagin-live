Questions

Before you start.

What exactly do I get?

A 15-page PDF: the 30-day roadmap, a decompression schedule, daily rhythm builder, trackers, checklists for setup and the first night, ten trust-building activities, and pages to prepare for your vet and trainer. You get it right after checkout.

My dog is already home. Is it too late?

No. Start from whatever day you're on. The rhythm works the same, and the kit says it plainly: skipped a day? Just pick the rhythm back up.

Is this a training program?

No. It's a practical plan of routines, checklists and trackers for your dog's first month home. It isn't veterinary care or behavior treatment. For health concerns or worrying behavior, bring in your vet or a qualified trainer. Page 11 helps you prepare for exactly that.

Phone or paper?

Both. Keep the PDF on your phone, or print the trackers and put the emergency page on the fridge.

Who do I ask if I have a question?

Message @tysonstime on Instagram. The humans behind the account reply.
