Recommended: Concept 2, Portal

Portal draws the brand promise in one picture: step through a door of light into your own movie and stand in the spotlight as the star. It holds both letters of I'M, survives 16px as a bold M with a bright core, and the door carries straight into Concierge and real estate, where opening doors is the job. For a more literal film-set feel, Concept 1 is the director's lens; its viewfinder corners can also frame the Portal in video intros.
